Three jobs for AI in the pipeline

Intelligent threat detection

Detect

Machine learning models what normal traffic looks like and flags what does not fit.

Automated security

Decide

A language model reads each finding in its code context, ranks it and drafts the fix.

Continuous compliance

Prove

Every build evaluates its controls and leaves the evidence behind.

AI advises. Policy decides. People stay accountable.

AI-Driven DevSecOps · ATAL FDP 2026

This slide is the map for the rest of the talk; the three columns are the three phrases in the title. Detect uses classical machine learning on runtime data. Decide uses a large language model on scanner output. Prove uses plain automation, with AI output as one input. Stress the last line: in our design the model never approves a release on its own. It produces a recommendation, a written policy makes the decision, and a person owns that policy. Come back to this slide when you reach each part.
