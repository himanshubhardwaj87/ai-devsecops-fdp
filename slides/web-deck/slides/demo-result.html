Come back to slides here to summarise what the audience just watched. The vulnerable app produced 24 findings after two duplicates were merged. AI triage on Bedrock judged 16 of them release-blocking: 10 at P0 and 6 at P1. It also lowered some: two outdated packages went down to P2 because the vulnerable code paths are not reached in this app. Every one of the five pinned packages had published vulnerabilities according to Amazon Inspector, and only 2 of 17 controls passed. After the fixes: nothing to review, all 17 controls pass, and the pipeline built the staging image and stopped for approval. The point of the last line: the decision to block or release did not depend on anyone's opinion on the day.
