AI-Driven DevSecOps · ATAL FDP 2026

This is the key idea of part two. Here is the login query from our demo app; the username goes straight into the SQL text. Bandit reports it at medium severity, which is fair from the tool's point of view: it matches a pattern and cannot tell whether the input is attacker-controlled. A human reviewer would look three lines up, see that username comes from the request form on an unauthenticated route, and call it the most urgent issue in the file. Triage is that step: reading the finding in its context. Until recently only a person could do it. A language model can now do a useful first pass, and that is where it earns its place in the pipeline.
