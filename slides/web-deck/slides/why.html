The problem

Code ships every day.
Security reviews do not.

Attackers already automate. A defence that waits for a human to read every alert loses on speed alone.

Open with a question to the room: how often does your department's project code get a security review? Once a semester? Never? Industry teams deploy many times a day, and a manual review cannot keep that pace. This gap - delivery speed versus review speed - is the reason DevSecOps exists, and the reason AI is now being pulled into it. Do not quote numbers here; let the audience supply their own experience.
